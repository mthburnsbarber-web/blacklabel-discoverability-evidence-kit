# Evidence Claim Checklist

Use this before adding a claim to a discoverability manifest.

## Claim quality

- The statement is specific enough to prove or refute.
- The source URL is public HTTPS.
- The source says the claim directly or contains the primary artifact.
- The review date is today or the date the source was actually checked.
- The claim does not depend on a private dashboard, local file, or memory.
- The wording avoids rankings, revenue, traffic, or customer counts unless the
  linked source supports the exact number.

## Strong source types

- Public product page.
- Public release note.
- Public case study with dated method and limits.
- Public changelog.
- Public documentation.
- Public dataset or API response with stable schema.

## Weak source types

- Screenshots without a URL or timestamp.
- Private analytics.
- Draft pages.
- Search snippets.
- Unpublished local files.
- Claims copied from a model answer.

## Maintenance

Review evidence again when:

- the product changes;
- the public URL changes;
- the claim mentions a number;
- the claim is older than the freshness window you set;
- a buyer, search engine, or AI answer reports a contradiction.
